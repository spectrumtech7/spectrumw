Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21/10/2026

Information We Collect

We collect only what you enter in our forms:

  • Contact form: your name, phone number, email address, message and location (if provided).
  • "Get in Touch" pop-up: your name, mobile number, profession, and what you are interested in (optional).
  • Buy Now button: we record which product was clicked. We do not collect your details on our website; you continue on WhatsApp, where our team can see the number you message from.

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and assist with your order.

How We Use Your Information

Your details are shared with our marketing team members to follow up with you directly, typically via WhatsApp or phone. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Your inquiry details are stored in our database and are accessible only to authorized Spectrum Technologies team members for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. Our website, database and uploaded files are hosted with third-party service providers who process data only on our behalf.

Cookies

We use small cookies to make the website work: one remembers that you have already filled in our "Get in Touch" pop-up so it is not shown again, and one keeps your shopping cart. Both last up to one year. We do not use cookies for advertising.

WhatsApp Communication

If you choose to continue your inquiry via WhatsApp, you will be communicating directly with a Spectrum Technologies team member. WhatsApp's own privacy policy governs that communication channel.

Your Rights

You may request that we delete your information from our records at any time by contacting us at spectrumtech7@gmail.com.
