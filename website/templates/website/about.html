Our Story

Spectrum Technologies was established in Vapi, Gujarat, on 22nd August 2017. The company began with a focused approach to developing customer relationships and introducing its lighting products directly to the market. In its early stage, product samples were personally delivered to prospective customers, helping establish the company's initial customer base.

Since then, Spectrum Technologies has grown into an established LED lighting manufacturing operation based at Vibrant Business Park, Vapi. Today, the company manufactures 200+ lighting products, supplies customers across India through its distribution network, and also serves as an OEM manufacturing partner for established brands such as Eveready and Jeep.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop and manufacture a diverse range of practical LED lighting solutions for distributors, retailers, decorators, and OEM customers. We focus on expanding our product range while responding to changing market requirements and customer needs.
